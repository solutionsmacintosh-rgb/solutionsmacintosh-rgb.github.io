Facebook Messenger AI agent for an Apple Authorised Reseller

A 24/7 Messenger assistant for an Apple Authorised Reseller store in Pampanga. It answers product, pricing and promo questions from the store's own knowledge base, and hands anything it can't handle to staff.

  • Meta webhook with token verification and an instant 200 OK, so Meta never retries
  • 5-second message batching merges rapid-fire texts into one question
  • Typing bubble shows the customer a reply is coming
  • Gemini main + backup model with automatic fallback and chat memory
  • 7 knowledge tools: store info, FAQs and promos (Docs), plus live promo and cash prices (Sheets)
  • Guardrails: never offers a promo before its start date; cash price only once the customer confirms
  • Staff handoff logs to Google Sheets and sends a Gmail alert
  • Fallback reply if the AI step fails, so no customer is left on read

FAQ

What time zones do you work in?
I'm based in the Philippines (GMT+8) and work flexibly with US, AU, and EU clients. For async work, time zones rarely matter — I keep you updated with clear written summaries and Loom videos.
Do you charge hourly or per project?
Both. One-time builds (CRM setup, automation flows, chatbots) are quoted per project. Ongoing support and executive assistance work on a monthly retainer. We'll agree on scope and pricing before anything starts.
What if I don't know what to automate?
That's exactly what the discovery call and audit are for. Tell me how your day-to-day runs, and I'll identify where automation saves the most time — prioritized by impact.
Will I be able to manage the systems myself afterward?
Yes. Every build comes with documentation and video walkthroughs so you or your team can operate it without me. Retainer support is optional, never required.

Send me a message with a quick description of your business and what's eating your time. I'll reply within 24 hours to book a free discovery call.
